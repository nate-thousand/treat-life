Brand Overview

Treat Life is a premium lifestyle dog brand built for design-conscious dog owners. This is not another pet food company.

Dog treats good enough to leave on the counter instead of hiding in the pantry.

Mission: Create premium dog treats worthy of premium homes.

Positioning: Premium nutrition + streetwear energy + modern design + collectibility + lifestyle.

Reference brands: Kith, Apple, Supreme, Liquid Death, Aesop — not PetSmart.

Product: Human grade dog treats — three launch flavors with flavor-specific accent colors.

Tagline: Live the Treat Life.

Target Customer

Not every dog owner. We build for people who already appreciate thoughtful design and premium consumer goods.

Their dog is an extension of their lifestyle.

PB & The Jams

  • Display: Druk, Bebas Neue, Monument Extended (Bebas Neue in POC)
  • Body: Inter, Neue Haas, Suisse (Inter in POC)
  • Flavor names: premium brush script — handwritten, not cartoon (Georgia italic stand-in)
  • Display headlines: uppercase, tight line-height
  • UI chrome: uppercase buttons, wide letter-spacing
  • Body: sentence case, muted gray on dark surfaces
  • No white background sections

Logo Direction

  • Large typography with a minimal icon — single premium mark
  • Preferred: shield + paw
  • No cartoon dogs, clip art, or excessive laurels
  • No crowns unless subtle
● Treat Life

Photography Direction

Do

  • Editorial, luxury, fashion energy
  • Hard shadows, natural light, warm tones
  • Luxury food styling
  • Surfaces: concrete, travertine, walnut, black marble

Do not

  • PetSmart-style pet aisle photography
  • Cartoon or overly cute dog stock
  • Farmhouse or rustic staging
  • Busy, cluttered product shots

Packaging Principles

  • Soft-touch matte pouch
  • Real metallic gold foil
  • Emboss and deboss
  • Spot UV accents
  • Large typography, minimal information hierarchy
  • Lots of whitespace — shelf-ready, collectible presence

Voice Rules

Use

  • Reward Better.
  • Live the Treat Life.
  • Human Grade. Dog Approved.
  • Good Dogs. Good Treats. Good Taste.
  • Designed for People With Good Taste.
  • Crafted for Good Dogs.

Avoid

  • Woof / Bark / Pawsome
  • Fur baby language
  • Cute pet puns
  • Pet store baby talk
  • Farmhouse / rustic tone
  • Cartoon energy

Approved Taglines

  • Live the Treat Life. — primary
  • Reward Better.
  • Crafted for Good Dogs.
  • Human Grade. Dog Approved.
  • Designed for People With Good Taste.
  • Good Dogs. Good Treats. Good Taste.
  • Real Ingredients. Real Loyalty.

Secondary taglines from brand handoff — use sparingly, not all at once.

Approved Product Names

  • Treat Life — brand name
  • PB & The Jams — Peanut Butter + Blueberry
  • Berry Fresh — Strawberry + Banana
  • Gold Standard — Sweet Potato + Honey

Forbidden Language

Do not use: Golden Flow (replaced by Gold Standard)

  • Do not use unverified regulated claims on public surfaces
  • Do not add Made in USA, no artificial, or AAFCO language until legal review
  • Do not use pet-aisle clichés listed in Voice Rules

Claim Review Notes

This is a concept site. Use concept language only until formulas, manufacturer, packaging requirements, and legal review are complete.

Verify before launch

  • Human grade
  • Made in USA
  • No artificial ingredients / no fillers
  • Ingredient benefits
  • Manufacturing partner
  • Nutritional and guaranteed analysis
  • Feeding guidelines
  • AAFCO, FDA, and state pet food label requirements
