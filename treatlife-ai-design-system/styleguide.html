Configuration Architecture

This site is a configuration-driven brand system. The visual design, layout, spacing, typography, and components are fixed. Brand identity, marketing copy, product data, and photography paths are separated into configuration layers so multiple brands can reuse the same design system without changing components or CSS.

Design System

Visual tokens live in tokens/treatlife.tokens.json, mirror to theme/treatlife.theme.css as --tl-* custom properties, and are consumed by styles.css. Components never hardcode hex values — they use token-backed CSS classes only.

Token categories: Colors, Typography, Spacing, Radius, Shadows, Borders, Gradients, Component, Naming.

Brand Configuration

Each brand concept in config/brand.concepts.js defines identity-only fields: name, tagline, logo, favicon, browserTitle, footerBrandName, socialPreviewImage, and copyright.

Components bind brand values through data-brand-name, data-brand-tagline, and related attributes. Marketing copy and product data stay shared across concepts.

Marketing Content

All user-facing copy lives in config/copy.js under structured keys: copy.hero, copy.about, copy.products, copy.cta, copy.footer, and more. The renderer binds copy to DOM nodes via data-copy-* attributes.

To launch a new brand on this system, swap the brand concept — not the component markup.

Product Data

Products are defined in config/products.js with id, name, flavor, description, imageKey, category, and card styling fields. The Product Grid component in js/components.js renders cards from this data — no hardcoded product HTML in pages.

Photography

All image paths are centralized in config/images.js as per-concept sets under assets/biggiebones/, assets/treatlife/, and assets/treatkings/. The Concept Switcher swaps the active image set instantly.

Component Architecture

Reusable section renderers live in js/components.js: Hero bindings, Proof Strip, Product Grid, Product Card, Copy System, CTA, and Footer. Class name contracts are documented in components/component-rules.md.

The homepage and style guide share the same Product Grid and Proof Strip markup — one source, no duplication.

Concept Switching

A developer-only Concept Switcher (bottom-right on the homepage) swaps the active brand configuration and asset set. Switching updates: logo text, brand name, tagline, browser title, footer brand name, favicon placeholder, and all photography from the matching assets/{concept}/ folder.

Layout, spacing, colors, product names, and marketing copy remain unchanged.

Product Detail Page

Product detail pages live at product.html?id={product-id} and render from config/products.js + config/copy.js via TL_COMPONENTS.renderProductDetail().

Each product includes: name, flavor/collection, hero image, short description, key benefits, ingredients, size, price placeholder, quantity selector, Add to Cart, Buy Now (concept only), nutrition details, shipping note, and related products.

Dog treats good enough to leave on the counter instead of hiding in the pantry.

Forbidden Language

Do not use: Golden Flow (replaced by Gold Standard)

  • Do not use unverified regulated claims on public surfaces
  • Do not add Made in USA, no artificial, or AAFCO language until legal review
  • Do not use pet-aisle clichés listed in Voice Rules

Claim Review Notes

This is a concept site. Use concept language only until formulas, manufacturer, packaging requirements, and legal review are complete.

Verify before launch

  • Human grade
  • Made in USA
  • No artificial ingredients / no fillers
  • Ingredient benefits
  • Manufacturing partner
  • Nutritional and guaranteed analysis
  • Feeding guidelines
  • AAFCO, FDA, and state pet food label requirements
