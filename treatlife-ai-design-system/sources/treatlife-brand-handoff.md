# Project Overview

Treat Life is a premium lifestyle dog brand built for design-conscious dog owners.

This is **not** another pet food company.

The vision is to create the **Kith / Aimé Leon Dore / Nike / Apple of dog treats** by combining:

- Human-grade ingredients
- Premium minimalist design
- Streetwear-inspired branding
- Collectible packaging
- AI-first marketing
- Limited product drops

Think:

> "The dog treat brand people display on their kitchen counter instead of hiding in the pantry."

# Long-Term Vision

Treat Life should eventually feel less like a pet food company and more like a premium consumer lifestyle brand.

If someone removed the words "dog treats" from the packaging, the design should still feel premium enough to sit next to brands like Apple, Aesop, or Kith.

Everything—from the ingredients to the unboxing experience—should reinforce one idea:

**Live the Treat Life.**
