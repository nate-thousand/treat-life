## Brand Summary

Treat Life is a premium lifestyle dog treat brand built for modern dog owners who care about ingredients, design, packaging, and the products they bring into their homes.

The brand is not trying to look like a traditional pet aisle product. It should feel closer to a premium consumer goods brand, with strong typography, collectible packaging, and a simple black and gold visual system.

Core idea:
Dog treats good enough to leave on the counter instead of hiding in the pantry.

## Brand Positioning
